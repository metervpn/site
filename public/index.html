Why it's safe

It can't take your whole network down.

The failure mode of most VPNs — flip it on, everything breaks — is designed out. MeterVPN Free routes by rule, not by default.

Default

Direct, always

Nothing routes through the VPN unless you asked for it. Unlisted traffic never touches the tunnel.

Selected

Only your sites

A local rules file sends just the domains you chose to your exit. Change them anytime, no reconnect.

Kill switch

One click back

Disconnect clears the routing instantly. If the tunnel ever drops, your direct traffic is unaffected.
